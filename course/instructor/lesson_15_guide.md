## Answers and detecting failures

**A1.** (i) Synthetic method result, bounded to the invented two-state
closed cell of Lesson 14. (ii) Project
motivation, not a dataset or prediction. (iii) **GAP**: Mey is a bulk
assessment bibliographic lead, not exact eligible runnable TDB bytes or
redistribution permission. (iv) **GAP** for this material claim: Fischer/
NIST is an atomistic lead for $\Sigma5$/$\Sigma3$, not matched [111]
excess validation. “Bibliographic/atomistic lead” is acceptable as the
record's role if its claimed *validation* is separately rejected.

**A2.** (a) Literature lead known; exact eligible binary bytes, rights,
unaries, reference/magnetic/phase/domain semantics GAP, so the bulk model
is not accepted. (b) No accepted stable FCC reservoir result or real $\mu_i$;
even a selected bulk model would still need equilibrium and independent
numerical checks. (c) Proposed [111] direction only; exact plane, angle,
count, area, reference and mechanical state GAP. (d) No matched one-state
free-energy/excess packet with uncertainty/rights. (e) No second matched
structural-state function; this additionally blocks transition scope.
(f) No held-out observation in the exact observable/geometry/domain.
The bulk source and geometry/one-state evidence block even a one-state
material prediction; the second-state evidence is an extra transition
blocker. A field can have several blockers; no arbitrary rank is required.

**A3.** Reject SGTE access→binary redistribution permission; paper citation
→complete exact TDB; EAM→CALPHAD reservoir; $\Sigma5$/$\Sigma3$→the
unfixed [111] boundary; source lead→$\Gamma$ result; solver jump→equilibrium
interface transition. A defensible replacement is: “The listed project,
assessment and atomistic records motivate a Ni–Cu boundary case and supply
source leads. The executable case is GAP pending an eligible bulk model and
matched boundary-state data; synthetic lessons can demonstrate methods.”
EAM and TDB are different model artifacts and physical roles.

**A4.** Observable: Cu Gibbsian excess in mol/m² at one fully specified
Ni–Cu boundary against a verified FCC reservoir. Supported claim: B06
motivates the question; Mey and Fischer/NIST provide separate literature/
atomistic leads. Bulk blocker: exact rights-cleared, complete versioned
Ni–Cu TDB that passes the bulk-model check. Boundary blocker: exact matched
[111] plane/angle/state/area/excess data that pass the matched defect-input
check. **GAP** is the only
admissible executable status. Conditional scientific interest is not the
`CONDITIONAL` status of an executable result.

**B1.** $G_{\rm cell}$ is J; each $\mu_i$ is J/mol atoms; each $N_i$ is
mol atoms; $A$ is m² per boundary. The numerator is J and $2A$ is m²,
giving J/m². Two equivalent boundaries must be the only boundary surfaces
being normalized; a free surface or inequivalent boundary needs its own
accounting. For a closed cell, fixed Ni and Cu amounts and mechanical
conditions lead to a constrained total Gibbs-energy minimum. The sketch
cannot be evaluated with the current evidence, and stressed-interface
excess terms need a separate physical contract.

**B2.** H1 might allow a separately checked **bulk** calculation; it lacks
matched boundary data and cannot predict $\Gamma$.
H2 supplies one matched state and could make a conditional one-state
segregation calculation eligible after the case, bulk and defect inputs are
accepted and a bounded real run is made; it cannot establish a two-state
transition. H3 makes a
two-state comparison question eligible in principle, but it still needs
compatible minimization/coexistence analysis, held-out material comparison,
independent checking and physical interpretation. It does not prove an
observed transition. These are hypothetical cards, not current evidence.

**B3.** (i) Different ensembles/reference/amount bases; compare one
appropriate potential under matched conditions. (ii) The two-boundary
cell excess must use the total relevant area $2A$; one $A$ doubles it.
(iii) A numerical discontinuity may reflect algorithm choice, metastability
or kinetics; equilibrium coexistence needs two matched state potentials,
appropriate interface thermodynamics and independent evidence. (iv)
Segregation equilibrium says nothing alone about strength or ductility;
mechanical-property data and a causal/mechanical model are separate.

**B4.** Accept a six-sentence memo with the actual source-audit GAP, a proposed
mol/m² question, the three real source roles, one concrete bulk blocker,
one concrete boundary blocker, synthetic-only teaching conclusion, and a
scoped case-selection next step (exact artifact/rights and geometry-candidate check).
For a transition, require two compatible, evidenced structural-state free-
energy functions and physical coexistence/held-out checks. Reject a memo
that silently imports H1–H3 as actual records, treats a missing value as
zero, or claims a real calculation has run.

## Handoff and readiness boundary

source-to-claim link.
The exact eligible Ni–Cu bulk artifact, boundary geometry/state/excess packet,

inputs, physical/resource/run controls and an independent check of the result
are prerequisites for any real calculation.
