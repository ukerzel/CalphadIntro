Timings are planned; they have not been observed with learners. Prepare log cards, four labelled

Each meeting is 90 minutes:85 contact/practice plus 5 break. No measured pace.
If logs stall, supply q values and repeat signs/reference reasoning; record the
support. If coding stalls, keep the paper route. Preserve break and independent
attempt; defer extra plot formatting instead of borrowing from the next lesson.

## Answers and error checks

A1: A0.8 mol, B0.2 mol, gref=−6600 J/mol. Changing the uniform composition to 0.5
changes both inventories. A2: AABB, ABAB, ABBA, BAAB, BABA, BBAA. Four sites are
an illustration; no finite-size entropy equals the stipulated macroscopic molar
formula. At 0.5 q=−0.69314718, Δs=5.763172 J/(mol K), Δg=−5763.172 J/mol;
at 0.2 q=−0.50040242, Δs=4.160596, Δg=−4160.596 in those units.
Both entropy changes are positive; ideal Δh=0. A3: use the zero product limit,
not ln(0); g=−9000 J/mol, h=1000 J/mol, s=10 J/(mol K). Epsilon changes inventory.

A4: Δh=0 and Δs=5.763172 J/(mol K) at both T; Δg=−3457.903 J/mol at 600 K and
−10373.710 J/mol at 1800 K. Total h=7000 J/mol, not zero.

B2: at 600 K, h=7000 J/mol, s=15.763172 J/(mol K), gref=1000 J/mol,
Δg=−3457.903340 J/mol, g=−2457.903340 J/mol. For 2 mol: H=14000 J,
S=31.526344 J/K, G=−4915.806680 J. Molar values do not scale with n; all totals
do. Changing T at fixed x changes Δg, not Δs or Δh in this model.

B3: initial 0.5 mol A+0.5 mol B, proposed 0.8 A+0.2 B. The proposal gained 0.3 A
and lost 0.3 B; fixed total amount alone is not component conservation. A curve
plot compares different homogeneous sample inventories, not a balanced search.

B4: q≈−0.56233514, Δs=4.675536 J/(mol K), Δg=−4675.535560 J/mol,
gref=−6000 J/mol, g=−10675.535560 J/mol, h=4000 J/mol. At 3 mol,
G=−32026.606679 J. For x=0.75, Δs and Δg match by reflection of q; h=10000,
gref=0 and g=−4675.535560 J/mol. The reference tilt adds 6000 J/mol between the
compositions. Equal entropy terms do not make unequal full reference energies equal.
