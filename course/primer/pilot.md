# Primer pilot — observation protocol

**Status: NOT RUN.** No participants, date, facilitator or observations have been
supplied. Blank fields below are an observation template, not missing values in
an otherwise completed study. This preparation cannot establish novice pacing
or learning effectiveness.

This observes the **one-day primer only**. The six-meeting opening lessons plus
Clinic A need a separate observation. Do not extrapolate this day to the full course.

## Arrange before delivery

The instructor needs an agreed date, delivery mode, facilitator and approximately
3–5 willing volunteers without CALPHAD experience, with varied thermodynamics/
coding preparation if feasible. Those are proposed arrangements, not recruitment
already performed. The instructor arranges participation. Use the normal local arrangements for voluntary participation.

Use the [README](README.md), [worksheet](worksheet.md),
[instructor sheet](instructor.md) and [hints/answers](answers.md). Record their
exact Git revision before running. Re-check any changed scientific explanation
or exercise before the pilot. Print a
sample in the actual renderer and inspect formulas, tables, units and minus signs.
Use the complete paper route by default; record any demonstrations separately.
Do not collect names, emails, individual profiles, audio/video or transcripts in
the repository. Publish only anonymous aggregate timings, support and task-level
reasoning summaries; omit identifiable anecdotes and small demographic breakdowns.

The four questions for observation are: can participants use conditions to choose
a potential; interpret the two lines including nonunique crossing fractions;
explain objective and valid amounts while rejecting unit errors; and distinguish
synthetic output from missing real-model inputs? We observe support needed, not
an unvalidated score or a claim of independent mastery.

## Run and record

Before 09:00, fill the logistics fields and prepare paper copies. Follow the
instructor timetable. At each block note actual start/end, approximate worked/
practice time, whether its task finished, and which hint stages were needed.
Use anonymous group counts for independently explained / explained with support /
not yet explained, with any non-attempt or missing observation explicit. Do not
infer successful understanding from a completed numerical table alone.

At W8, use fresh 1050 K and both error cards before giving feedback. At W10 use
975 K and all four exit actions with the reference sheet. Record named assistance
(reference only, hint 1/2, partner, answer revealed) in aggregate. A correct answer
after revealing it is supported work, not an independent exit explanation.
Feedback belongs within its allocated minutes; do not omit unfinished actions.

If behind, transfer twenty minutes from W9 to core work, then switch optional
live code to paper outputs. Record which recovery was used and the changed
start/end times. Keep both breaks, lunch and W10. If core still does not fit,
record incomplete outcomes and redesign another offering; no “pass” by dropping
conditions or conservation. Stop optional software debugging after an unexpected
result and mark the supplied-output route.

## After the observation

Keep corrections to numbers or sources separate from pilot findings. Link each
recurring misconception, repeated rescue or overrun to a specific task/explanation.
Propose one focused change, say how you will check that it helped, and specify
which segment needs another observation. Record unexpected issues too. A small successful usability sample
supports only the limited observed context; it does not establish general teaching
effectiveness. Unobserved activities remain unobserved.

Future evidence should state: exact course version, actually delivered scope, attendance
count, observed times/hints/explanations, recovery decisions, incomplete actions,
necessary revisions and rerun plan. Do not replace this blank template with invented
results. Publish a separate dated aggregate record.
