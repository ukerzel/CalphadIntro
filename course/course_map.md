# Course map

One page for finding your way. Hours are estimates and have not yet been
measured with learners.

## Pick a goal

| Goal | Do this | About |
|---|---|---|
| The primer outcomes | [Day 1](primer/README.md) and [Day 2](primer_day2/README.md) on paper, or self-study steps 00–02 and 04 | two days in class, or 6–10 hours alone |
| The advanced steps | from materials science: the primer outcomes, then [step 07](day3/README.md); from operations research: steps 08–09 | plus 2 hours, then 30–40 minutes per step |
| Real alloys | the primer outcomes, then step 03 parts A–C (or the [two-phase sheet](primer_day2/two_phase_sheet.md)), steps 05–06 and [Tasks 01–05](README.md) | plus 1–2 days |

Everything stays open whatever goal you pick; the goal only says what you
need to finish.

## The parts

- **Day 1** (paper; "Basics on paper" on the website): energies, the
  lowest-G rule and one invented element with a solid and a liquid.
- **Day 2** (paper; "Mixtures and boundaries on paper"): a binary curve,
  chemical potentials, and a grain boundary in an open or a closed cell.
- **Self-study steps 00–06** (website): the same ideas with labs, then two
  phases (step 03) and two real alloys, Cu–Ni and Ni–Nb (steps 05–06).
- **Advanced steps 07–18** (website and notebooks, optional): the
  equilibrium calculation read as an optimisation problem; for learners from
  materials science (start at step 07) or operations research (start at
  step 08). See the [overview of the advanced steps](day3/README.md).
- **Tasks 00–05** (notebooks): worked examples with published databases.

## Junctions

- Before Task 01: the two-phase sheet, or step 03 parts A–C (about 30
  minutes).
- Before step 10: step 07, or steps 08–09. Step 03 parts A–D are
  recommended, not required. The optional LP primer (before step 10) is for
  anyone who has never met a linear programme.

## Short paths

Short of time? These paths keep the main idea of each part (about thirty minutes for each day on paper, about two hours for the advanced steps):

- **Day 1:** choose the potential (W3), the two lines and their crossing
  (W4), and what the optimiser may change (W6).
- **Day 2:** count A and B (D1), the tangent and its end heights (D3), and
  open against closed (D5–D6, the worked numbers only).
- **Advanced steps:** step 10 (the menu), step 12 (the gap curve) and step 14
  (ceiling and floor), main track only.
