# Day 2 model, source and lesson map

This primer adapts the course's own **synthetic** material. No external
database, Ni–Cu parameter or new solver is included.
The [first primer](../primer/README.md) supplies the fixed-$T,p$ Gibbs
choice and unary comparison; Day 2 starts at its W9 missing-input map.

The full binary course includes nonideal stability, same-model tool checks and
one-parameter fitting in [Lessons 7–9](../assembled_route.md). Day 2 names
those topics as further study; it does not imply that an ideal ALPHA example
is a complete real bulk assessment. The [assembled route](../assembled_route.md)
is the one-page map to all readings, tasks and instructor answers.

The binary contract's I1 model and the synthetic boundary contract govern all
numeric definitions in the learner sheet. The sheet writes the boundary
contract's per-boundary sites $S$, area $A$ and boundary count $K$ as $N_s$,
$a_{\rm gb}$ and $n_{\rm gb}$ (values unchanged), and calls I1 ALPHA “the ideal
ALPHA model”. On the learner sheet, D8's four evidence steps are case and
source, bulk model, boundary model, and calculation and comparison.
After the primers learners continue with the worked examples, Tasks 00–05
([course index](../README.md)): D1–D3 → Task 01, D4–D6 → Task 04, D7 → Tasks
03/04; the lessons above are optional depth.

Optional primary background references are already cited in the relevant
contracts and lessons. Required tasks depend only on the equations, numbers
and reference card printed in this primer. This map records sources and
deeper reading, not extra learner homework during either primer day.
