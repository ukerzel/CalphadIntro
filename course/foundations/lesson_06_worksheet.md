## Meeting 14 — addition versus exchange

**A1 — worked paths.** Start nA=nB=0.5 mol. Add 0.01 mol B at fixed A, then instead
exchange 0.01 mol A for B at fixedtotal. List final nA,nB,n,x for each. Which path
does g′ describe? Which path defines μB?

**A2 — guided total-energy change.** Given g=−8763.172233, g′=12000 J/mol at x=0.5,
calculate μA,μB. Estimate the first-order ΔG for a0.001 mol B addition and for a
0.001 mol A-to-B exchange. Explain why opposite signs are consistent.

**A3 — derivative diagnostic.** Someone differentiates g with respect to x and
labels 12000 “μB”. Supply the missing reference/normalization term using G=ng.
If code uses one tiny finite-difference step, what additional check is needed?

**A4 — independent exit.** At x=0.2 ALPHA, supplied g=−10760.595951 and
g′=473.655534 J/mol. Calculate μA,μB and verify g=(1−x)μA+xμB. State precisely
which amounts remain fixed for each chemical potential and for the exchange slope.

## Meeting 15 — tangent and global support

**B1 — worked line.** Write ALPHA's tangent at x=0.5 as c+mx. Evaluate it at x=0,1
and 0.8. Do the values at 0 and 1 equal the pure phase's g values? Why or why not?

**B2 — guided rejection.** At x=0.8 BETA has g=−10760.595951 J/mol. Compare to B1's
line and explain why ALPHA-only support is insufficient for the I2 problem.

**B3 — guided proof.** If every allowed gφ(x)≥c+mx, multiply by phase fractions
and sum. Which two balances reduce the result toc+mz? How does a touching balanced
split meet that lower bound? Which argument is missing from a sampled plot?

**B4 — independent exit.** The I2 common tangent at 1000 K has c=−10762.730017, m=0.
Add 432−765x J/mol to both phases. Give new μA,μB,slope and the energy shift at z=0.25.
Do endpoints/amounts change? Would the same conclusion hold if only BETA shifted?

## Meeting 16 — Clinic B: fresh integrated attempt

Retrieve definitions first, then attempt these with support labels. No universal
score establishes readiness; record the explanation as independent/supported/not yet.

**C1 — denominators.** A2 mol sample has z=0.4. Supplied endpoints xα=0.2, xβ=0.8.
Find phase mole fractions and amounts, then each component inventory. Explain why
BETA's amount fraction is not 0.8. The supplied endpoints are a trial, not certified.

**C2 — addition/exchange.** At one homogeneous state x=0.25, supplied g=−10000 and
g′=2000 J/mol (a separate algebra example, not the I2 branch). Find μA and μB;
estimate ΔG for adding 0.002 mol B and for replacing 0.002 mol A by B.

**C3 — unsupported candidate line.** A proposed line is ℓ(x)=−11000+2000x J/mol.
At x=0.8 an allowed phase has g=−10760.595951. Does the line support all states?
Compute the violation. Do not assume the proposed line is tangent anywhere;
one violated state is sufficient to reject its global-support claim.

**C4 — explanation handback.** On one page distinguish (i) feasible amounts,
(ii) lower energy than tested candidates, (iii) global supporting-line proof,
(iv) a real-material validation claim. Give one item of evidence each would need.
Then describe why a grid can pass both balances yet still miss the continuous
minimum. Identify one concept to revisit before adding nonideal interactions.
