### Hints to reveal after an attempt

1. A1/A3: a DOI, file download and model licence are three different
   questions. Compare **geometry** and **physical quantity** as well as alloy.
2. A2/A4: step 1 selects the exact case/source; step 2 checks the bulk;
   step 3 checks matched defect input. One boundary state cannot answer a
   two-state question.
3. B1/B3: if $\mu$ is J/mol, $N$ must be mol. Ask which variables are fixed
   before choosing the potential or an area denominator.
4. B2/B4: “eligible to attempt” still requires a bounded run, independent
   numerical/scientific acceptance and a relevant held-out comparison before
   a material conclusion.
