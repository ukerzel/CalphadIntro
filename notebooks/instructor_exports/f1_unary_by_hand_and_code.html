3. What may an optimiser change? (W6)¶

Let f_L be the liquid fraction and f_S = 1 − f_L, both between 0 and 1. At fixed T and p the optimiser minimises $g_{mix} = (1 - f_L)\,g_S + f_L\,g_L$. It may change the fractions, never the model. The cell below shows the idea at 1100 K; your turn is at 900 K.

In [6]:
def g_mix(T, f_liquid):
    return (1 - f_liquid) * g_solid(T) + f_liquid * g_liquid(T)

print("f_L:", [0, 0.5, 1], "→ g_mix(1100 K) =", [g_mix(1100, f) for f in (0, 0.5, 1)], "J/mol")

f_L: [0, 0.5, 1] → g_mix(1100 K) = [-10000.0, -10300.0, -10600.0] J/mol
